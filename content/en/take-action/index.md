# Practical steps

This section collects practical, low-friction actions you can take when AI systems affect decisions about you, your organisation, or the services you rely on.

The goal is not activism. It is clarity and follow-through: understand what applies, request the right information, keep records, and know when to escalate. AI should serve people, and asking good questions is one of the simplest ways to make sure it does.

**Where things stand (6 November 2026):** the AI Act's bans, AI literacy duty and transparency rules (for example, chatbots must say they are AI) already apply. The main high-risk rules for AI in hiring, credit, benefits and education apply from 2 December 2027. Until then, GDPR is your strongest tool for automated decisions. See the [AI Act timeline](/en/timeline).

## Quick start

Pick one:

- **5 minutes**: check the [timeline](/en/timeline) and note where AI appears in your life or workflow
- **15 minutes**: use a template request letter and customise it
- **30 minutes**: write a short risk/impact note (what the system does, who it affects, what could go wrong, and who can step in)
